-- General Market Backend
-- Migration: 000002_market_domain_foundation.sql
-- Additive, versioned and non-destructive.
--
-- Ownership boundaries:
-- * No account credentials or authoritative role database.
-- * No free-balance ledger or authoritative reservation ledger.
-- * No official property ownership register.
-- * No legal contract body owned by Contracts service.
-- * No service-delivery records owned by Services.
-- * No final settlement ledger owned by Settlement.
--
-- External identifiers are stored as references, not copied records.
